## What's new in 0.2.0-alpha.3

### From player feedback
- **Result card fixed:** after loading a model, pressing Analyze or ending a game no longer leaves the game result on screen instead of the analysis. The card belongs to the scored move and goes away with Analyze or a new game. Ending a game by two passes returns to the analysis layout, as resigning does.
- **Settings at any size:** with a small interface scale, a high-DPI screen or after zooming, Settings no longer spills its controls past its right edge.
- **Zoom with dialogs open:** Ctrl+= and Ctrl+− work while Settings or another dialog is open.
- **Move dialogs:** drag any dialog (Settings, New game, Edit theme, Customize layout, Updates…) by its title bar.
- **Shift+click a point:** on a stone, jumps to the move that placed it; on an empty point, jumps ahead to where the current branch plays there.
- **Mouse buttons as shortcuts:** bind the middle and thumb buttons in Settings → Keys. By default the thumb buttons step back and forward through the moves.
- **Paint stones:** in Markup, drag with the black or white stone tool to place stones on every empty point you pass (start on a stone of that colour to remove them instead).
- **Settings → Thresholds:** every cut-off that hides or classifies data is now yours to set — the "% chance a player of this rank plays it" for human moves, which candidates are shown, territory marks, what the estimate counts as decided or likely, the mistake / blunder levels (the report's colours and filters follow them), Hawk Eye Match, the report's "unlikely for this rank" level, and where the middle game and endgame start.

### Also new
- **Theme editor:** a "Start from" picker loads any built-in or saved theme to edit.
- **Board sync on Windows:** the screen is captured with desktop duplication, so boards shown over browser video (hardware overlays that used to read as black) are seen. The board picker shows a still snapshot of the screen instead of covering the live one, and reading a board after captures is more reliable.
- **Rank estimates for many games** (`gifu --rank`): games are fed to KataGo in a sliding window, about 2.6× faster.
- **Leela:** analysis can be capped at a number of playouts.
